# M6 dynamic groups and account lifecycle

Contract baseline: 19 October 2026. Apply V7 explicitly after V6; startup never migrates a working database. M1–M5 packets, epochs, recovery and personal state remain compatible.

Groups contain 2–100 distinct configured, admitted, verified accounts at creation and may retain one member afterwards. Names are trimmed, 1–100 UTF-16 units. Only the owner renames, transfers ownership, adds or removes members. Members may leave; the owner first transfers ownership unless alone. Direct blocks have no group effect. Group mutation requests have a 60/hour/account budget; preparation is bounded to 120/hour/account. UUID operation IDs are durable per account, with a fingerprint of the complete immutable request; matching retries return a minimal recorded result and differing requests conflict. Metadata revisions are independent of membership revisions.

Creation prepares a client-selected room UUID and the complete recipient snapshot. Addition prepares the complete future snapshot with membership revision incremented once. Both commit a fresh PLK1 epoch and complete envelopes atomically with membership. No identity or missing-backup error changes membership. Removal/leave revoke membership immediately, advancing membership revision; current clients resume ordinary automatic epoch activation. No send can use the former epoch. Rename/owner transfer do not rotate keys.

Current membership intervals gate history, files, previews, unread, export and saved-send results. Rejoining sets JoinedSequence to the committed LastSequence + 1, resets personal history/read state, and starts a new membership revision. Envelope access also checks JoinedMembership, preventing access to an earlier epoch when departure/rejoin occurred without messages. Historical intervals and author references remain for integrity.

V7 assigns existing encrypted groups to the lowest admitted, verified participant ID and preserves their intervals, ciphertext, envelopes and personal state. Groups without an eligible owner close. Legacy dbo chat data stays in its original tables, clearly separate from encrypted history; original timestamp timezone remains unknown and no UTC conversion is invented. Import of legacy group membership requires an explicit reviewed mapping and admitted, configured accounts; plaintext bodies are never imported as encrypted messages.

Approved lifecycle policy: deactivation suspends admission/sessions without changing group membership or ownership. Account deletion removes own message/image bodies and vault copies, retains neutral sequence positions and operation tombstones, revokes membership immediately, closes direct rooms, and assigns owned groups to the lowest remaining eligible account ID or closes them. A deletion ledger must be retained outside restored backups and replayed before serving a restored installation. M7 must rehearse this process and agree production backup retention. Local copies already obtained by participants cannot be erased remotely.
